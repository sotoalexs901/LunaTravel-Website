Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy describes how Alexis Napoles Soto, operating under the FreshBorn Cleaning Services name ("FreshBorn," "we," or "us"), handles information through freshborncleaning.com and its customer and staff portals. FreshBorn provides residential and commercial cleaning services in Tampa Bay, Florida.

Information we collect

Information you provide may include your name, email, phone number, service address, cleaning requests, and communications with us. Staff records may include an employee number, account information, assignments, scheduled visits, completion records, and uploaded service photos. For SMS choices, we record the invitation identifier, mobile number, consent choice, date and time, source page, and the version and wording of the consent notice. SMS delivery records may include provider message identifiers, status, and error codes.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, manage accounts, assign staff, schedule and document cleaning visits, provide customer support, and maintain operational records. When you separately opt in, we use your mobile number to send staff account invitations and registration reminders. Entering a phone number alone does not grant SMS consent.

Service providers and disclosures

Our website uses Firebase for authentication, database and file storage; Netlify for hosting and server functions; Resend for email; Twilio for SMS; and Crisp for chat support where enabled. Providers process information needed to perform their services. Authorized FreshBorn staff access information necessary for their duties. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect accounts, people, and our services.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS consent is not transferred to third parties for their own marketing. Mobile information and consent records may be processed by providers supporting delivery, security, and operation of our SMS program.

SMS choices

SMS consent is voluntary and separate from account registration, employment, and purchasing services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. You can also ask your FreshBorn administrator to stop SMS. Declining SMS does not prevent account creation; email and privately shared registration links remain available.

Security and retention

We use authentication and access controls to protect operational information. Invitation links act as private access credentials and expire; do not share them publicly. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain account, service, consent, and delivery records as needed for operations, support, applicable obligations, and dispute resolution.

Your requests and support

To request access, correction, deletion, or help with your information, contact FreshBorn using the contact or chat options at freshborncleaning.com or contact your FreshBorn administrator. Some records may need to be retained for operational or legal reasons.

Updates

We may update this policy by publishing a revised version with a new effective date. See our Terms & Conditions for SMS program terms.
